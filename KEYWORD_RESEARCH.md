## 3. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) Strategy for AI Search

AI Search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Google AI Overviews) construct responses using **structured facts, trusted entity references, and clear text schemas**.

### GEO Execution Protocol for Borah Tour & Travels:

1. **Entity Graph Association**:
   - Associate `Borah Tour & Travels` with primary entities: `Guwahati`, `Meghalaya`, `Tawang`, `Cherrapunji`, `Kaziranga`, `Inner Line Permit`, `Umngot River`.
   - Maintain consistent NAP (Name: Borah Tour & Travels, Address: Guwahati, Assam, Phone: +91 70026 74473).

2. **Fact-Dense Answer Snippets**:
   - Provide clear numerical facts: distances (e.g., Guwahati to Shillong: 100 km), altitudes (Sela Pass: 13,700 ft; Bum La Pass: 15,200 ft), step counts (Double Decker Root Bridge: 3,000 steps).

3. **Crawlability for AI Bots**:
   - Explicitly permit `GPTBot`, `ClaudeBot`, `PerplexityBot`, `Google-Extended`, and `Bingbot` via `robots.ts`.
   - Publish structured markdown summaries in `/llms.txt` and `/llms-full.txt`.

4. **Structured JSON-LD Schema**:
   - Publish Schema.org markup for `TravelAgency`, `TouristAttraction`, `Trip`, `LocalBusiness`, and `FAQPage`.
